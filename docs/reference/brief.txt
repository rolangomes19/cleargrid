                   Product Design Case Study Brief

For Product Design Candidates




Overview

This case study brief invites product design candidates to redesign the
Borrower Profile Page within ClearGrid's collections CRM platform. The
objective is to improve visual hierarchy, data grouping, and overall usability
while creating a design that works seamlessly in both English (LTR) and
Arabic (RTL) layouts.

The current interface is live and functional but has evolved organically over
time. This presents an opportunity to apply fresh UX thinking and design
clarity to create a more efficient, agent-friendly experience.



About ClearGrid

ClearGrid is a next-generation debt resolution partner helping institutions
improve recovery, reduce cost-to-collect, and enhance customer experience
across the full DPD (Days Past Due) spectrum. Using predictive AI, omni-
channel orchestration, and real-time portfolio intelligence, ClearGrid creates a
resolution process that is faster, fairer, and more consistent for lenders and
borrowers alike.

ClearGrid operates primarily in the UAE and KSA (Saudi Arabia) markets,
serving clients across banking, BNPL, telecom, digital SMEs, and embedded
finance sectors.

Redesign Requirements

Your Redesign Should Include

1. Redesigned UI Layout (Desktop Only)

Create a comprehensive redesign of the Borrower Profile Page for both
English (LTR) and Arabic (RTL) versions. The design should prioritize agent
efficiency and quick access to critical information.

2. Improved Visual Hierarchy

Demonstrate clear thinking about which information should be prominent,
grouped, collapsible, or tabbed. Consider the agent's workflow and prioritize
information accordingly.

3. Component-Based Thinking

Design with modularity in mind. Create reusable components such as cards,
tables, accordions, and action buttons that can scale across the platform.

4. UX Rationale

Provide a brief explanation (2-4 bullet points or a short Loom video)
describing your thought process and how your redesign improves usability for
collections agents.
